---
id: INV-003
statement: The server treats client-supplied designs and inputs as untrusted: block set, types, damage, pose and input axes are validated or clamped before use, and peer identity comes from the transport, never the payload.
criticality: high
evidence:
  - POL-server-validates-client-design
---
Rationale: ServerSimulation.Join builds the authoritative ship from the client's ShipSnapshot
(arbitrary blocks, TypeId, Mods, Damage, position, velocity, angular velocity, unbounded count),
and the replicated snapshot is rebroadcast to every peer. SetInput accepts sbyte axes that
decode to -1.008 (unclamped). NetId is `(ushort)peer`, which truncates and can collide.
Enforced by scripts/check_unity_tree.sh check 8.
