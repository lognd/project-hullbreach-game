Planets are now terrain with rules: crashes scale with relative normal speed above a safe landing threshold and hurt only the touching blocks (the core is spared unless the hit is catastrophic), a thin atmosphere drags and heats fast ships, ship physics substeps so nothing tunnels, planet damage is now broadcast to clients, the HUD can read `ShipBody.QueryPlanetProximity()`, and everything is tunable through the new `planets` block of the arena config.
