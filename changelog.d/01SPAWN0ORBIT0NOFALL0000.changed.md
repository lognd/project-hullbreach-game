Ships now leave the match spawns with a launch velocity (4 u/s, almost straight up) instead of starting at rest, so an idle ship no longer falls onto the big planet within 21 s; it stays clear of every planet for about 95 s and the approach to the fight is clear. The spawn line is 140 units wide and 22 units below the arena centre; `spawn.speed` and `spawn.launchDegrees` tune the launch (speed 0 restores the at-rest spawn).
