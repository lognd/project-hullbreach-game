---
id: INV-001
statement: Wire decoding in Hullbreach.Net never trusts remote bytes for sizes or offsets; every read is bounds-checked against the received length and no allocation is sized by an unvalidated wire value.
criticality: high
evidence:
  - POL-net-no-remote-sized-alloc
  - POL-net-reader-bounds-checked
---
Rationale: ByteReader indexes the raw buffer with no length check, ClientReplica.ApplyReceived
ignores its `length` argument and reads stale bytes, ShipSnapshot.Read allocates
`new SnapshotBlock[count]` from a remote u16, and unknown kind bytes fall into the reliable path.
A hostile or corrupt peer can throw IndexOutOfRange on the receive path or force allocations.
Enforced by scripts/check_unity_tree.sh check 8. Fix ticket anchors `frob:invariant INV-001`.
